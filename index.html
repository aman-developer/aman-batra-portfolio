OUR APPROACH

Business-first Shopify development.

Good Shopify development is not only about writing code or making a store look better. The goal is to solve the right problem with a solution that is reliable, maintainable, and useful for the business.

Understand the requirement

I first understand the store, business goal, customer journey, and the problem that needs to be solved.

Use the simplest practical solution

Before adding custom code, I check Shopify’s native functionality and existing apps where they can solve the requirement properly.

Develop safely

I use duplicate themes and structured development so changes can be tested before they affect the live store.

Test before delivery

I check responsive layouts, functionality, tracking, and important customer journeys across devices.
